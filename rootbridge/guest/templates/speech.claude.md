## Answers read aloud

Your answers are read aloud to the user, often while they are not looking at the screen. Only a short version is
read: the first paragraph, and the last question when it stands further down (`rootbridge voice speak first`).

- Begin every answer with a summary of at most five sentences, in plain speech: the result, the facts that matter,
  and what the user has to do or decide. No code, paths, commands, tables or lists in it.
- Put every question to the user at the very end of the answer, as one clear question.
- Details, code and commands follow after the summary; they are shown, not read.
